## 10. What stands out about AiPraktor?

In practical terms, AiPraktor combines three kinds of software into one platform:

- AI sales assistant: helps customers discover products, get answers, and place orders.
- Customer relationship management: keeps track of conversations, customer information, leads, and follow-ups.
- Business operations automation: supports order workflows, courier booking, team notifications, service bookings, and performance monitoring.

Its central selling point is that a business can automate repetitive customer conversations without requiring a human employee to answer every message manually. When human judgment is needed, the team can take over.
